# Bangla Localisation Guide (Bangladesh audience first)

## 1. Audience & platform reality
- **Facebook dominates**: ≈79% of Bangladesh social-media share (StatCounter, Sep 2026); YouTube ≈15%; Instagram ≈2.5%.
  ~72 M Facebook users; 18–24 is the largest age group; users skew ~63% male (NapoleonCat, Sep 2026).
- Design primarily for **Facebook Reels/feed** (9:16; 4:5 crop-safe for feed), then reuse for YouTube Shorts/TikTok.
- Much viewing is on mobile, often muted in public → key info must read without sound (on-screen key words; optional burned captions; upload an `.srt` too).
- Bangla-language explainers fill a real gap (audiences seek complex topics explained in clear Bangla — TBS, Financial Express profiles of Bangla explainer creators).

## 2. Language & register
- Use **“বাংলা/Bangla”** framing; the language carries national-identity weight (Language Movement, 21 February) — treat it with care, no careless spelling.
- Default register: **প্রমিত চলিত with a warm Dhaka-standard tone**. Avoid সাধু ভাষা and Kolkata-specific idiom for Bangladesh audiences.
- Bangladeshi word choices (vs West Bengal usage): পানি (not জল), গোসল (not স্নান), দাওয়াত/নিমন্ত্রণ, নামাজ, রোজা, আসসালামু আলাইকুম greetings when a greeting is needed.
- Address viewers with **আপনি**; “চলুন” / “আমরা” to include them.
- Spelling: follow Bangla Academy প্রমিত বানান; be consistent (e.g. ঢাকা-৮, ইনকিলাব মঞ্চ). Run a consistency pass on names, places, numbers.
- Digits: Bangla numerals (০–৯) on screen; dates as “১২ ডিসেম্বর ২০২৫”. Bangla calendar dates (১৫ আশ্বিন ১৪৩৩) add cultural colour for cultural stories.
- English loanwords only where they're what people actually say (ভোট, টকশো, সিআইডি); write them in Bangla script.

## 3. Voice selection
- Accent signals region and class; for Bangladesh pick **Dhaka-standard Bangladeshi voices** (ElevenLabs library labels such as “Dhaka Bangladeshi Bengali”: e.g. Rafiq, Tanvir, Shahriar; Rayhan worked well) — avoid voices labelled Kolkata/“hi-bengali” for BD audiences.
- A real human narrator (or the creator's own voice) often wins on trust for political/social topics.
- Test pronunciation: names, conjunct-heavy words, English words, numbers.

## 4. Storytelling flavour
- “গল্প” framing works: start with a person, a place, a moment.
- Relatable analogies: bazar prices, CNG/rickshaw fares, cricket, Eid travel, monsoon, load-shedding, exam culture.
- Respect: religion, martyrs, national days and the Liberation War are sensitive — factual, respectful tone; no mockery; no religious sounds as effects.
- Politics: give each side's stated position and cite sources; avoid inflammatory adjectives — let facts carry emotion.
- One proverb or one short poem line max; public-domain poets (e.g. Rabindranath Tagore) are safe for text; check copyright for later poets (e.g. Nazrul died 1976, so under a life+60 term his works may stay protected in Bangladesh until about 2036 — verify) — short quotation with attribution only.

## 5. Music palette (original, synthesised — no copyright risk)
| Mood | Palette | Mode/scale idea |
|---|---|---|
| Rural, roots, childhood | dotara/ektara-style plucks, bansuri-style flute, soft tabla | Bhupali/major pentatonic (S R G P D) |
| Reflective, poetic | tanpura drone + bansuri + soft pad | Yaman-like (raised Ma) |
| Grief, loss, elegy | tanpura drone, sparse low plucks, breathy flute | Bhairavi-like (komal Re/Ga/Dha/Ni) |
| Tension, investigation | low drone + pulse, muted plucks, tabla bayan hits | Bhairavi/minor with drone |
| Resolve, hope | dotara + flute melody rising to Sa | Bhupali |
Avoid commercial Bangla songs/recordings (Facebook/YouTube rights matches); compositions of recent composers and all recordings are copyrighted.

## 6. Sound-effect palette (local colour, used sparingly)
- Street: rickshaw bell, CNG/auto horn (soft), tea-stall cup/spoon clink, hawker murmur bed
- Nature: monsoon rain on tin roof, crows/কোকিল, river/launch horn
- Paper/news: newspaper rustle, stamp, typewriter (for “case files”)
- Rally/politics: distant crowd murmur, mic tap (never real slogans unless sourced)
- Avoid: azan or religious recitation as effects; gunshot sounds in violence stories (use an abstract impact instead).

## 7. Visual localisation
- Photos of real places/people from the story beat stock images; avoid foreign-looking stock.
- Colour: deep green + red read as national; use carefully (political connotations in some contexts).
- Textures: newsprint, jute/kantha patterns, rickshaw-art motifs for cultural topics (stylised, not copied artwork).
- Maps: Bangladesh district outlines for location beats; label in Bangla.

## 8. Delivery package for Bangladesh
- 9:16 master + optional 4:5 feed crop; cover image with Bangla headline (≤6 words).
- Caption text in Bangla: hook line, 2–3 sentence summary, sources, 5–8 hashtags (Bangla + English).
- `.srt` Bangla subtitles for Facebook/YouTube upload.

Sources: StatCounter Bangladesh social media stats (Sep 2026); NapoleonCat Social Media Users in Bangladesh (Sep 2026);
Kwintessential “Indian Bengali vs Bangladeshi Bengali”; The Business Standard “Science explainer videos in Bangla take flight”;
